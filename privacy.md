# Time Tracker Mobile Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 25, 2026**

Tariq Sekhri ("we", "us", or "our") provides Time Tracker Mobile (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App accesses, collects, uses, stores, and shares information.

## Information the App accesses and collects

Time Tracker Mobile accesses the following information only after you grant Android's Usage Access permission:

- **App usage and activity data:** the apps used on your device, package names, app labels, activity events, usage start and end times, durations, and foreground/visible-use statistics.
- **Installed-app information:** app names, package names, versions, installation/update times, and whether an app is a system app. This allows the App to identify and categorize the apps represented in your usage history.
- **App settings and content you create:** categories, skipped-app patterns, display preferences, and the usage logs created by the App.
- **Optional sync information:** if you choose to configure a sync server, the server address you enter, a device name, a generated device identifier, and an authentication token.

The App does not collect your contacts, messages, photos, files, precise location, microphone, camera, payment information, advertising ID, or health data. It does not show advertising and does not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

## How information is used

Usage and installed-app information is used to create, display, filter, categorize, and manage your personal time-tracking log. By default, this information is stored locally in the App's private database on your device.

Sync is optional. If you configure a server and register the device, the App periodically or when you request it sends that server the app name/package identifier, timestamps, duration, a device identifier, and the authentication token necessary to associate the data with your registered device. The App uses this information only to provide the sync feature. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or sale of data.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal or sensitive user data. The App does not share data with advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties. The only transfer made by the App is to the sync server address you choose and configure. The operator of that server is responsible for its storage and access controls.

## Data retention and deletion

Local usage logs, categories, skipped-app patterns, and sync settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. You can delete individual logs, clear all logs, remove skipped-app entries, or change/remove the configured server from within the App.

When sync is enabled, deletions of synced logs are sent to the configured server during the next successful sync. To remove a registered device and the data held by a sync server, contact that server's administrator. Because the App does not provide a hosted user account, it has no account-deletion process separate from deleting the App's local data and asking the selected server administrator to remove server-side records.

## Security

The App stores its local database and settings in Android app-private storage. Sync credentials are stored in the App's private preferences and authentication tokens are stored as hashes by the compatible server. The current sync feature connects to the server address you provide; use only a server and network you trust. Do not configure an untrusted or public network endpoint.

## Your choices

You may decline or revoke Usage Access at any time in Android Settings. Without Usage Access, the App cannot import usage history. You may use the App without configuring sync. You can clear local data through the App's controls or by uninstalling the App.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated when we do.
